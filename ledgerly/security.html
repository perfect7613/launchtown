Security & Trust

Your financial data, defended in depth.

Ledgerly holds the most sensitive data a startup has. Here is — specifically and verifiably — how we protect it.

🛡️ SOC 2 Type II attested 🔐 AES-256 at rest · TLS 1.3 in transit 🏦 Read-only bank access via Plaid 🗑️ 30-day verified deletion

🛡️ SOC 2 Type II

Ledgerly maintains a SOC 2 Type II attestation covering the Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust service criteria. Unlike a Type I snapshot, Type II means an independent auditor (Prescient Assurance) observed our controls operating effectively over a continuous 12-month audit window — most recently completed March 2026.

  • Controls are monitored continuously via Vanta; drift triggers an engineering page, not a quarterly review.
  • The full audit report is available under NDA to any customer or prospect — email security@ledgerly.app and we'll send it the same business day.
  • We also complete an annual third-party penetration test (most recently by Cure53, January 2026); an executive summary is available on request.

🔐 Encryption, at rest and in transit

In transit

  • All traffic — browser to Ledgerly, and Ledgerly to every upstream provider — is encrypted with TLS 1.3 (TLS 1.2 minimum, older protocols disabled).
  • HSTS is enforced with preloading; plaintext HTTP never serves content.
  • Internal service-to-service traffic is mutually authenticated (mTLS) inside a private VPC — nothing listens on the public internet except the load balancer.

At rest

  • All databases, object storage, and backups are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Encryption keys live in AWS KMS with automatic annual rotation; application servers never hold raw keys.
  • Especially sensitive fields (account numbers, aggregator tokens) are additionally encrypted at the application layer, so even a direct database compromise yields ciphertext.

🏦 Read-only bank access, via Plaid

Ledgerly connects to your bank exclusively through Plaid, the same regulated aggregator used by Venmo, Robinhood, and thousands of financial institutions. This design has three properties worth being precise about:

  • We never see your banking credentials. You authenticate directly with your bank inside Plaid's flow. Your username and password are never transmitted to, stored by, or visible to Ledgerly — we receive only a scoped access token.
  • Access is strictly read-only. The token Ledgerly holds is scoped to the transactions and balances products only. It cannot initiate payments, move money, or modify anything at your bank. There is no code path in our system that can move your funds.
  • You can revoke it at any time. Disconnect from your Ledgerly settings, from your bank's connected-apps page, or via Plaid Portal — revocation takes effect immediately and we purge the token within minutes.
Why this matters: even in a worst-case, full-compromise scenario of Ledgerly's systems, an attacker could read transaction history but could not move a single dollar. We deliberately architected away the ability to touch your money.

🗑️ Data deletion policy

Your data is yours, and leaving is easy — we think lock-in via hostage data is a bug, not a retention strategy.

  • Self-serve export: download your complete ledger, attachments, and reports as CSV/PDF at any time, including after you cancel.
  • Deletion on request: request deletion from settings or by emailing privacy@ledgerly.app. Production data is hard-deleted within 72 hours; encrypted backups age out completely within 30 days. We send you written confirmation when both are complete.
  • Automatic offboarding: if your subscription lapses, we retain data for 90 days (in case you return), then delete it on the same schedule above.
  • Aggregator cleanup: deletion requests are propagated to Plaid so your item and its tokens are removed there too.

🧭 Operational security

  • Access control: production access requires hardware-key MFA (FIDO2), is role-scoped, granted just-in-time, and every session is logged and reviewed. No standing admin access to customer data — support access requires your explicit, time-boxed consent.
  • Infrastructure: hosted on AWS (us-east-1, with cross-region encrypted backups), infrastructure-as-code, immutable deploys, and audit logging shipped to a write-once store.
  • Development: mandatory code review, dependency and secret scanning on every commit, and staging environments that never contain real customer data.
  • People: background checks for all employees, security training at onboarding and annually, and offboarding that revokes all access within one hour.

📣 Vulnerability disclosure & contact

We want to hear from security researchers. If you believe you've found a vulnerability:

  • Email security@ledgerly.app — PGP key and our full disclosure policy are published at /.well-known/security.txt.
  • We acknowledge reports within 24 hours, triage within 72, and keep you updated through remediation. Good-faith research is protected under our safe-harbor policy.

For anything else — audit reports, security questionnaires, DPAs, or a call with our security lead — email security@ledgerly.app. Median first response is under 4 business hours.
